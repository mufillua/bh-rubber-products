What are industrial safety products?

Industrial safety products are the equipment that protects people from injury at work — in factories, steel plants, workshops, warehouses, construction sites and kitchens. Most of it is PPE, short for personal protective equipment: things a worker wears, such as safety gloves, safety shoes, helmets, goggles and coveralls.

PPE is the last line of defence. It works alongside guarded machines, safe working methods and training, not instead of them — but where a hazard can't be removed, the right PPE is what stands between the worker and an injury.

What are Type 5 and Type 6 coveralls?

“Type” numbers come from the European classification of protective clothing against chemicals. Types 1 to 4 cover gases, vapours, and jets or sprays of liquid chemicals. The two you hear about most for coveralls are:

  • Type 5 — protection against hazardous dry particles, such as fine dust and fibres (EN ISO 13982-1).
  • Type 6 — limited protection against light sprays and small splashes of liquid chemicals (EN 13034).

A “Type 5/6 coverall” meets both, and is usually a disposable suit. The coveralls on this site are cotton and poly-cotton workwear coveralls (boiler suits) for general industrial work — they are not Type 5 or Type 6 chemical protective clothing.
